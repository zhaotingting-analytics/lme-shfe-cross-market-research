# lme-shfe-cross-market-research
Quantitative research on cross-market dynamics between LME and SHFE base metal futures

## Research Objective

This project investigates cross-market price dynamics between London Metal Exchange (LME) and Shanghai Futures Exchange (SHFE) copper contracts.
The analysis focuses on whether price dislocations between the two markets,after adjusting for foreign exchange rates and contract specifications,exhibit statistically persistent or mean-reverting behaviour.
The project combines commodity-market structure with quantitative methods including time-series analysis, spread modelling, signal research, backtesting and risk analysis.

## Research Questions

1. How closely do LME and SHFE copper prices move after FX and unit adjustment?
2. Is the adjusted cross-market spread stationary?
3. Does the relationship change across volatility and market regimes?
4. Can deviations from the long-run relationship generate systematic signals?
5. Do potential signals remain robust after transaction costs and risk controls?
